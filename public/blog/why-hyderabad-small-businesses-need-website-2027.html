2. Why "Rented Land" (Instagram & Aggregators) Is Failing Local MSMEs

Many entrepreneurs say: "I already have 3,000 Instagram followers and a Google Maps pin. Why should I spend time setting up a website?"

In technology strategy, relying on Instagram or third-party marketplaces is known as "building your house on rented land":

A. Organic Reach on Social Media Has Collapsed

In 2027, organic reach for business posts on Instagram and Facebook hovers below 3.8%. That means when you post an update or new collection, 96 out of 100 followers never see it unless you pay to boost the post. Furthermore, if your account is flagged or restricted by an automated algorithm, your entire sales pipeline vanishes overnight with zero human support.

B. Aggregator Commissions Are Eating Up Margins

Food delivery aggregators, salon platforms, and B2B directories now take up to 28% to 35% commission on every order. For an independent restaurant, cloud kitchen, or boutique service in Hyderabad, that is often your entire net profit. Having your own website with a 1-tap WhatsApp ordering channel lets you keep 100% of your earnings.

C. The Lead Reselling Trap of Directories

When a customer submits an inquiry on Justdial, IndiaMART, or Sulekha, the directory broadcasts that lead via SMS to 4 or 5 competitors simultaneously. This triggers an immediate price war where you are forced to discount your service just to win the customer. On your own website, every lead belongs exclusively to you.
